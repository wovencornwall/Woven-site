The fine print

Terms & privacy

Clear, simple terms, so you always know where you stand.

About these terms

WOVEN is the trading name of Zoë Meads, a wedding planner based in Cornwall. These terms explain how I work with you. When you book, you'll receive a booking agreement setting out your date, your service, your price and when payments are due. If anything in your booking agreement differs from these terms, your booking agreement applies.

Enquiries and proposals

Our first call is free and there's no obligation. Afterwards I'll send a tailored proposal and quote within three to five days. Your quote is valid for 30 days.

Booking and payment

  • Your date is secured once I've received your signed booking agreement and a 50% deposit.
  • The balance is paid after your event, on the date set out in your booking agreement.
  • Travel is quoted separately, depending on where your celebration is, and is shown in your quote.
  • Any additions you choose are charged as agreed in your quote.

Changes to your plans

Plans change, and that's fine. Please let me know as early as you can. If you'd like more time or services than we agreed, I'll confirm the cost with you before going ahead.

If your date moves, I'll do everything I can to move with you. If I'm free on your new date, your deposit moves with it. If I'm not, the change is treated as a cancellation.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel, please tell me in writing. Your deposit is non refundable, because it holds your date and I turn other couples away for it. No further payment is due unless your booking agreement says otherwise.

In the unlikely event that I can't be with you because of illness or an emergency, I'll do everything I can to arrange a trusted, experienced coordinator in my place. If that isn't possible, I'll refund everything you've paid me.

Suppliers and venues

I'll recommend and coordinate your suppliers, but your contracts are with each supplier directly, and I'm not responsible for the goods or services they provide. On the day I'll always do my best to solve any problem that comes up. I'll follow your venue's rules, and ask that everyone involved does the same.

Food, drink and licensing

I don't sell or serve alcohol. Where alcohol is provided at your celebration, licensing is the responsibility of your venue or caterer. Any menu and wine guidance I give is advice to help you and your caterer decide.

Your belongings and décor

I'll take great care with everything you entrust to me. I can't be responsible for loss of or damage to items left at the venue, unless it's caused by my own negligence.

Liability

My liability to you is limited to the fees you've paid me for your service. Nothing in these terms limits liability where the law doesn't allow it, such as for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

Things outside anyone's control

If something beyond our control, such as severe weather, serious illness or travel disruption, affects your celebration, we'll work together to find a new date or the best way forward.

Photos and kind words

With your permission, given in your booking agreement, I may share photographs and your kind words on my website and social media. You can change your mind at any time by letting me know.

Privacy

When you send an enquiry, I receive your names, email address and anything you choose to tell me about your plans. The form is delivered to my inbox by a service called FormSubmit.

  • I use your details only to reply to you and, if you book, to plan your celebration.
  • I never sell or share your details for marketing.
  • I keep enquiries only as long as I need to, and booking records for as long as the law requires.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time.
  • This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device's internet address in order to send them.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, I'm always happy to talk it through. Email me at zoe@wovencornwall.com.
