SewnFrom £650 + travel

For couples who have planned it all and want someone who truly knows them to hold the day.

Your venue and suppliers are booked and the details are chosen. What's missing is someone who knows your story as well as your schedule. So we start with you: how you met, where you both come from, the people who matter most, what you hope the day will feel like and the life you're building beyond it.

From six weeks before, I become your point person. I learn your plans inside out, make sure every supplier knows what's happening and when, and shape the timeline around what matters to you. On the day I run everything quietly in the background, so you're never answering questions or watching the clock.

You've done the planning. Now let it be held.

What's included

  • Your story session: time to get to know you both, your families and friends, and what you hope for, in person or by video
  • Email support from the day you book
  • Two planning calls to go through every detail
  • A venue walk round together
  • Supplier confirmations in the final weeks
  • Your wedding day timeline, shaped around your story
  • Up to ten hours on the day as the main contact for your suppliers and guests
  • Your personal touches set out with care
